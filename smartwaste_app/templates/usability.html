System Usability Scale (SUS)

10 short questions about this app. 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree. It takes about 2 minutes. Your answers help the project measure usability (target: score {{ cfg.susTarget }} or more).

How the score works

Odd questions are positive, even questions are negative. The answers become one score from 0 to 100. A score of 68 is average; 80 or more is excellent.

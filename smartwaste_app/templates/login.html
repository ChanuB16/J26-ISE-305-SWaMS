Welcome to SmartWaste LK

Smart bins that tell us when they are getting full, forecasts of how much waste is coming, and truck routes that change when the roads do.

  • Officers see every bin live, the forecast and the routes, and get early warnings.
  • Drivers get their next stop and mark bins as emptied.
  • Citizens find the nearest bin with space, report problems and earn green points.
